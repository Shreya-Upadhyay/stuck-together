stuck together

flippable scrapbooks you make with your friends: for someone's birthday, a trip, a whole year, or just the group. everyone adds photos, notes and stickers.

how it works

  1. start a scrapbook for a person, a trip, a year, or the group itself.
  2. send the invite link to the group chat. friends sign in and they're in.
  3. fill it together with photos, tickets, letters, lists and stickers. everyone sees changes live.
  4. keep adding to it whenever something happens, or give it to the person it's for.
